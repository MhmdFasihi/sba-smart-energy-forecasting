# Smart Energy Forecasting: results report

_Generated by `src/final_eval.py` from `results/test_results.json`; checked by `src/check_report.py`._

Task: **day-ahead** forecast of the German day-ahead electricity price (ct/kWh) and solar generation (MWh per hour) for day D+1 (00:00-23:00), made at 12:00 of day D (auction cut-off, 12-36 h ahead), with a low (10 %) / middle (50 %) / high (90 %) band, from the last 168 h before 12:00 of day D. Data: SMARD (Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de, CC BY 4.0); the price is built from the native 15-minute day-ahead prices (hourly mean); weather from DWD observations (Open-Meteo for the last weeks, same time convention). Split: train 2019-2024, validation 2025, test 2026-01-01 to 2026-11-16 (320 forecast days). Each model is trained with 3 seeds (mean ± std over seeds).

## Protocol and model selection (validation only)
- Input ablation (seed 0, mean validation pinball loss over LSTM/CNN/PatchTST): base 0.0841; + weather 0.0850; + known-future inputs 0.0437; + weather + future 0.0437; + within-hour 15-min price range 0.0429. Decision: known-future inputs (official SMARD day-ahead solar / wind / load forecasts and calendar of the target day) used; 15-min range feature used.
- Hyper-parameter grid (learning rate × dropout, validation loss, seed 0): LSTM: lr 0.001, dropout 0.1; CNN: lr 0.001, dropout 0.2; PatchTST: lr 0.0003, dropout 0.1.
- Selection: lowest mean validation pinball loss over seeds → **PatchTST** (0.0420 ± 0.0008); next: PatchTST + weather 0.0425, CNN 0.0430. The test set was scored once afterwards (`final_eval.py` refuses to run twice without `--force`) and was not used for any choice. The app default is the best seed of the winner (`patchtst_future_intra_final_s0`).

## Findings
- Price: 4 of 6 neural variants beat the best naive baseline (naive day D (price only), pinball 1.191) on test pinball loss; best variant PatchTST (0.832), worst LSTM (1.540).
- Solar: 6 of 6 neural variants beat the best naive baseline (naive last day (D-1), pinball 979.427) on test pinball loss; best variant LSTM + weather (285.759), worst PatchTST (331.056).
- Official SMARD day-ahead solar forecast (benchmark, its band comes from training residuals): MAE 719, pinball 252. Best neural solar MAE: LSTM + weather 862; best neural solar pinball: LSTM + weather 286 → no neural model beats the official forecast on solar, although it is one of their inputs.
- Weather effect on price pinball (without → with weather): LSTM 1.540→1.212 (-21.3 %, within seed noise); CNN 1.141→1.050 (-8.0 %, within seed noise); PatchTST 0.832→0.977 (+17.4 %, larger than seed noise).
- Weather effect on solar pinball (without → with weather): LSTM 286→286 (-0.0 %, within seed noise); CNN 314→302 (-3.9 %, within seed noise); PatchTST 331→321 (-3.2 %, within seed noise).
- Validation winner PatchTST ranks 1 of 6 on test price pinball and 6 of 6 on test solar pinball; ranks among top models are close and rest on 3 seeds.
- Band calibration (target 0.80): price coverage 0.44-0.66, solar 0.68-0.80.
- Winner `patchtst_future_intra_final_s0` vs naive last day (D-1), price MAE by month: worse in 1 of 11 test months (month 11) (see `test_mae_by_month.csv`).
- Calibration: coverage was already below the 0.80 target on validation (price 0.64-0.72, solar 0.70-0.82) and is lower on test (price 0.44-0.66, solar 0.68-0.80): the bands are overconfident, most for price. No recalibration (e.g. conformal prediction on the validation year) was applied.

## Deviations from the guide
- Day-ahead protocol (12:00 cut-off) instead of forecasting from midnight; known-future inputs added (see ablation).
- PatchTST is a native PyTorch implementation, because the neuralforecast version is univariate (no exogenous inputs).
- CNN uses kernel 4 (dilations 1-32, receptive field 190 h) so the last step sees the whole 168 h week, 6 layers as in the guide but **128 filters** (not the guide's 64): all three models share one width setting (128 = LSTM units / CNN filters / PatchTST d_model). LSTM: 2 layers, 128 units as in the guide.
- Training: Adam, batch 64, up to 50 epochs, early stopping after 5 epochs, as in the guide; the learning rate and dropout were chosen on validation from the grid above (so the learning rate is not always 0.001). Trained on a Mac (Apple MPS), not Google Colab.
- Libraries: charts in the app use Altair (Streamlit built-in) instead of plotly; neuralforecast is not used (see above).
- The LLM explanation uses Anthropic or NVIDIA (optional), with a template fallback without API key.

## Limitations
- 3 seeds and one split; differences of a few percent between models are within noise.
- Weather was trained on observations, not forecasts, so the solar gain would be smaller in real use (guide section 9). The last weeks of weather come from Open-Meteo (modelled values), not DWD.
- Timing caveat for the known-future inputs: SMARD stores one value per hour of the official day-ahead solar / wind / load forecasts, not the vintage available at 12:00 of day D. If a forecast was last updated after 12:00, the models see slightly better forecasts than a real noon run would, so the test scores are optimistic. The ablation without these inputs (validation loss about 0.08 vs 0.04) shows how much of the skill depends on them. This could not be verified from the data and should be checked before real use.
- The 'naive day D (price only)' baseline was added after the test set had been scored once (it needs no tuning and changes no model choice); `final_eval.py` was re-run with `--force` only to add it and to correct report wording. All model predictions are unchanged.
- Negative prices and 2022 produce large errors. Costs in the planner use wholesale prices only (no taxes or grid fees).
