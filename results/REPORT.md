# Smart Energy Forecasting: results report (v3)

_Generated by `src/final_eval.py` from `results/test_results.json`; checked by `src/check_report.py`, which also recomputes the test metrics independently from the raw prediction and data files._

Task: **day-ahead** forecast of the German day-ahead electricity price (ct/kWh) and solar generation (MWh per hour) for day D+1 (00:00-23:00), made at 12:00 of day D (auction cut-off), with a low (10 %) / middle (50 %) / high (90 %) band, from the last 168 h before 12:00 of day D plus known-future inputs of the target day. Data: SMARD (Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de, CC BY 4.0), hourly price = mean of the 15-minute day-ahead prices; weather history from DWD observations (Open-Meteo for the last weeks). Split: train 2019-2024, validation Jan-Aug 2025 (early stopping, all choices), calibration Sep-Dec 2025 (band recalibration only), test 2026-01-01 to 2026-11-16 (320 forecast days). Every final model is trained with 5 seeds (mean ± std over seeds).

## Version history (read first)
- v1 forecast from midnight; v2 introduced the 12:00 cut-off. Both versions scored the 2026 test year, so **2026 is not an untouched hold-out any more**: the v3 changes below were motivated by reviews of v2, partly by its test results. Within v3, every decision is taken on validation, and the selection rule was written to `experiment_plan.json` before the v3 test scoring.
- v3 fixes: (1) the 15-minute price-range input used in v2 was constant (0) for the whole training period (SMARD repeats the hourly price before the 15-minute market started in Oct 2025) and is removed; (2) the noon-safe models now get the day-D auction prices of the target hours (public at 12:00 of D), which v2 withheld; (3) each input family is tuned separately with 2 seeds per setting and the finals use 5 seeds; (4) Diebold-Mariano tests; (5) solar quantiles are clipped at 0 and solar is also scored on daylight hours; (6) the band recalibration uses a separate calibration split and per-hour widening; (7) CNN with the guide's 64 filters; gradient clipping and an LR scheduler.

## Input families
- **[noon-safe]** (primary result): calendar of the target day, the official day-ahead **load** forecast (due two hours before the day-ahead exchange closes, SMARD manual 04/2026) and the **day-D prices** of the same hours (public at 12:00 of D).
- **[all fc]** (upper bound): additionally the official day-ahead **wind and solar** forecasts, which are only due 'by 18:00 of the previous day'. Their version at 12:00 is not available in the data, so these models may use information a real noon forecast would not have.

## Protocol and model selection (validation only)
- Weather ablation [noon-safe] (lr 1e-3, dropout 0.1, mean over 3 models x 2 seeds): without weather 0.0785, with weather 0.0776 → weather used (rule: used only if it lowers the loss by more than 1 %).
- Weather ablation [all fc] (lr 1e-3, dropout 0.1, mean over 3 models x 2 seeds): without weather 0.0404, with weather 0.0410 → weather not used (rule: used only if it lowers the loss by more than 1 %).
- Hyper-parameters (lr × dropout grid, 2 seeds each, chosen per family and model): LSTM [noon-safe] lr 0.001 / dropout 0.2; CNN [noon-safe] lr 0.0003 / dropout 0.1; PatchTST [noon-safe] lr 0.001 / dropout 0.2; LSTM [all fc] lr 0.0003 / dropout 0.1; CNN [all fc] lr 0.001 / dropout 0.1; PatchTST [all fc] lr 0.0003 / dropout 0.1.
- Selection rule (primary result = 'safe' family (inputs available at 12:00 of day D); 'allfc' = upper bound. Winner = lowest mean validation loss over 5 seeds within the safe family. Fixed before any v3 test scoring.) → **PatchTST + weather [noon-safe]** (validation loss 0.0721 ± 0.0015); next: CNN + weather [noon-safe] 0.0767, LSTM + weather [noon-safe] 0.0789. Best of the upper-bound family: PatchTST [all fc] (0.0394 ± 0.0007). The app default is the best seed of the winner (`patchtst_weather_safe_lr0.001_do0.2_s0`).

## Deviations from the guide
- Day-ahead protocol with a 12:00 cut-off instead of forecasting from midnight; known-future inputs of the target day.
- PatchTST is a native PyTorch implementation (the neuralforecast version is univariate, no exogenous inputs).
- CNN: 6 layers, 64 filters as in the guide, kernel 4 (dilations 1-32, receptive field 190 h ≥ 168 h). LSTM: 2 layers, 128 units as in the guide.
- Training: Adam, batch 64, up to 50 epochs, early stopping after 5 epochs as in the guide, plus gradient clipping (norm 1) and halving the learning rate after 2 epochs without improvement; learning rate and dropout tuned on validation. Trained on Apple MPS, not Google Colab (`colab_training.ipynb` runs the same chain).
- Validation is Jan-Aug 2025; Sep-Dec 2025 is reserved for band recalibration.
- Charts in the app use Altair instead of plotly. The LLM explanation uses Anthropic or NVIDIA (optional), with a template fallback.

## Limitations
- The 2026 test year was already scored in v1 and v2 (see version history); a fully fresh hold-out would need data after 2026-11.
- 5 seeds and one split; the DM tests treat the seed-averaged forecast as one forecaster.
- Weather enters only as observed history up to D 11:00 (no weather forecast for D+1), so it carries little information about tomorrow. Archived weather forecasts with a day-ahead lead time were checked: the Open-Meteo previous-runs archive starts in 2024, too late for the 2019-2024 training period.
- The [all fc] scores depend on the official wind/solar forecasts, whose vintage at 12:00 is unknown (see Input families).
- Timestamps are local wall-clock time from SMARD: the repeated October hour is dropped and the missing March hour is filled, which shifts a few windows by one hour around the clock changes. Holidays are national only.
- Negative prices and the 2022 crisis produce large errors. Planner costs use wholesale prices only (no taxes or grid fees).
